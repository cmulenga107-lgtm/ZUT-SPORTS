PLAYER UPDATE

Chisha expected to miss two months

Super Boys defender Chisha is expected to be sidelined for approximately two months following an injury. The ZUT Premier League Committee visited him to show support and wish him a speedy recovery.

The league has urged players to prioritise safety, respect one another and maintain fair play throughout the competition.

Status: Expected return in approximately two months.
