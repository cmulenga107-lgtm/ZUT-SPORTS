Building teamwork on and off the pitch

ZUT Sports Editorial • Sample article

⚽

Sport is about more than the final score. It can help students practise communication, discipline, cooperation and leadership.

Working towards a shared goal

In a team environment, every player has a role. Players learn to listen to one another, support teammates and keep working when a challenge arises. These habits can also be useful in group assignments and everyday campus life.

More than the game

Training can encourage time management, commitment and respect. A supportive sports community also gives students an opportunity to meet others and build friendships.
